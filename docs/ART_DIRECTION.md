# Art direction — illustrated worlds and manga scenes

## Runtime maps

NEON, KAGE and CITADEL now use authored Canvas layouts in `src/world-renderer.js`. They no longer load the three generated whole-map paintings. Paving uses the actual navigation rectangles from `src/levels.js`; six individual illustrated building sprites are placed on authored footprints. Narrow alley structures, foliage and street details are drawn natively. Antialiasing replaces the previous nearest-neighbour presentation. Smooth actor artwork is in `src/entities.js`.

KAGE uses stone paving, layered temple roofs, paper lanterns, torii, bamboo-coloured woodland, sakura and water. NEON uses restrained teal harbour streets, workshops and canal bridges. CITADEL uses angular prison blocks, warning accents and reactor channels. The old paintings are retained under `public/art/worlds/` as reference assets.

## Animated portraits and faction

`public/art/characters/` contains 36 transparent WebP portrait frames for 18 people, each with a closed and speaking mouth pose. `src/characters.js` selects these raster frames; robots retain original SVG portraits with speech lights. Their mouths animate while subtitles reveal. This is text-driven animation, not recorded dialogue or audio lip synchronisation.

The original pirate faction is **De Gebroken Zon**. Its broken eclipse and descending fractured blade are drawn in `public/art/pirate-sigil.svg`. The mark is not a copied Berserk emblem. It appears during the kidnapping and enemy transitions.

### Prologue atlas

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a production manga story atlas for the original sci-fi adventure Project X. Create one high-resolution landscape image with EXACTLY six EQUAL square panels in a flush 3-column by 2-row grid, no gutters or frames, no text, no lettering, no captions, no speech balloons, no watermarks. Each panel is a separate finished illustration that can be cropped out. Cohesive premium inked manga / anime visual-novel art, deliberate thin ink contours, confident anatomy, restrained smooth cel shading, elegant composition, little texture. NOT pixel art, not photorealistic, not painterly noise or artificial tiny pseudo details. Consistent cast: the young boy age 9 with black tousled hair, orange scarf and teal flight jacket; his mother with dark bob hair and cream flight coat; his father with brown swept hair and teal engineer coat. Pirate faction called Broken Sun uses an ORIGINAL crimson broken eclipse / three-pronged downward sword sigil, never copy any manga logo. Panel order left to right: 1) inside a warm family home overlooking a distant futuristic city, both parents kneel to embrace the frightened child as red warning light spills in; 2) on a cargo dock at night, mother urgently helps boy climb into a small white and teal cargo explorer ship while father opens the hatch, camera intimate; 3) close view through the ship window, boy places his hand against glass while both parents stand outside waving goodbye, visibly separate on opposite sides of glass; 4) black-armored SPACE PIRATES seize the two living parents on the dock under a huge shadowy pirate cruiser with a crimson eclipse sigil, no gore; 5) the white/teal cargo ship escapes into space above the attacked planet with a burning atmospheric horizon, sense of scale; 6) years later the same protagonist now age 17 discovers a bright cyan handheld Omni-Tool in an opened crate inside that cargo ship, face lit by its light. Keep major subjects within each panel's middle 80%, well separated silhouettes. The grid order and six specific narrative scenes are critical. Request maximum detail and resolution without pixelation.
