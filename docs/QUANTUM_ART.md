# Quantum Districts — asset record

All four atlases were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Each output was inspected, split at its exact equal-cell boundaries and encoded as WebP; no new facial or paint edits were applied. Original designs are inspired by the requested genres without copying characters, insignia or creatures from other games.

| Workspace paths | Frames | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/art/characters/{nyx,cass,flux}-{closed,open}.webp` | 6 × 512 × 512, RGBA | NEON speaking portraits |
| `public/art/characters/{akari,sumi,kaede}-{closed,open}.webp` | 6 × 512 × 512, RGBA | KAGE speaking portraits |
| `public/art/enemies/{chorus,matriarch,warden,bellkeeper,moth,fox}.webp` | 6 × 512 × 512, RGBA | Battle illustrations and world bosses |
| `public/art/story/{district-neon,dungeon-neon,district-kage,dungeon-kage}.webp` | 4 × 768 × 512 | Narrative backdrops |

Portraits use quality 90, enemies quality 90 and story panels quality 92. All portrait and enemy alpha channels are preserved. The originals deliberately use different mouth poses; only the active speaker’s mouth alternates. Three extra enemy families (prismawachter, oni and prismaschaduw) use original authored Canvas/SVG designs in `src/enemies.js`. Paving, collision geometry, dungeon layouts, foil effects, UI themes and motion are authored in code.
